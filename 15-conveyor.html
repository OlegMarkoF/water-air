Ocean Circulation

The Global Ocean Conveyor Belt hypothesis explains the core principles of ocean water circulation from the surface to the seafloor. This museum installation offers a simplified representation of that three-dimensional system, highlighting two “coolers” in the North Atlantic and the Antarctic alongside a single “heater” in the low latitudes of the World Ocean (the tropical–equatorial zone).

Deep-water formation

North Atlantic: Labrador Sea, Norwegian Sea, Greenland Sea.
Antarctic: Ross Sea, Adélie Coast, southern and western Weddell Sea, Amery Ice Shelf.

Upwelling

Occurs around the entire Antarctic ring.

The Global Ocean Conveyor Belt (GOC)

  1. Influences Earth’s climate and its variability.
  2. “Switches on” in the high southern latitudes.
  3. Driven by variations in seawater density and winds along the southern edge of the Antarctic Circumpolar Current.
  4. The classic GOC hypothesis does not incorporate water circulation within the Arctic Ocean.
  5. Its circulation pathway truly resembles a giant conveyor belt.
  6. While the Earth completes a rotation in 24 hours, water takes hundreds or even thousands of years to complete a full circuit along the conveyor belt.
Walk across the floor of the World Ocean, where the main branches of the ocean conveyor belt come to life around you!

大洋环流

全球大洋传送带假说阐释了海水从海面至海底的环流主要原理。本次博物馆装置是这套三维运动系统的简化演示模型，模型包含：两处“制冷区”——北大西洋与南极；一处“加热区”—— 世界大洋低纬度区域（热带-赤道带）。

海水下沉区域

北大西洋：拉布拉多海、挪威海、格陵兰海。
南极：罗斯海、阿代尔海岸、威德尔海南部与西部海域、埃默里冰架。

海水上涌区域

分布于整个南极环带。

全球大洋传送带

  1. 影响地球气候及其多变性。
  2. 在南半球高纬度海域“启动”。
  3. 驱动力来自大洋内部海水密度差异，以及南极绕极流南缘的风力作用。
  4. 当前全球大洋传送带假说并未纳入北冰洋水体环流。
  5. 大洋传送带示意图形态确实如同传送带的带状结构。
  6. 地球自转一周仅需24小时，而水体完成一轮传送带循环，却需要数百年乃至上千年。
