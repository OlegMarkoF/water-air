All About Earth’s Oceans

The ocean is worth writing about just as man is.Ernest Hemingway

Pacific Ocean

Historical names: South Sea, Eastern Ocean, Great Ocean

Area: ≈ 178,680,000 km² · Volume: ≈ 710,360,000 km³

Max depth: 11,022 m · Avg depth: 3,976 m · Salinity: 32–36.5‰

Temperament / Climate: Ranges from subarctic to temperate

Distinguishing features: Largest and deepest ocean on Earth. Abundant volcanic and coral islands, Largest concentration of islands (Oceania), Pacific Ring of Fire, Highest number of recorded tsunamis, Violent hurricanes and typhoons, Deepest ocean trenches, Deepest point on Earth.

Atlantic Ocean

Historical names: Sea of Atlantis, Western Ocean, Outer Sea, Hesperian Sea, Atlantic Sea, Sea of Gloom, Ocean Sea, Sea of Darkness, Oceanus Occidentalis, Oceanus Atlanticus

Area: ≈ 91,660,000 km² · Volume: ≈ 329,660,000 km³

Max depth: 8,742 m · Avg depth: 3,597 m · Salinity: 35.3‰

Temperament / Climate: Ranges from subarctic in the north to temperate in the south

Distinguishing features: Second-largest and second-deepest ocean on Earth. Few seamounts, guyots, and coral reefs; no fringing reefs, Longest mid-ocean ridge (the Mid-Atlantic Ridge), Highest tidal range in the world (Bay of Fundy), Highest number of inflowing rivers, Saltiest surface waters of any ocean, Major coastal upwelling zones

Indian Ocean

Historical names: Erythraean Sea, Southern Sea, Azanian Sea, Indikon Pelagos (Indian Sea), Sea of the Indies, Oceanus Orientalis Indicus

Area: ≈ 76,000,000 km² · Volume: ≈ 280,000,000 km³

Max depth: 7,729 m · Avg depth: 3,700–3,900 m · Salinity: 30–36‰

Temperament / Climate: Ranges from equatorial to subtropical

Distinguishing features: Located predominantly in the Southern Hemisphere, Longest chains of coral islands, High sea surface temperatures, Low coastal indentation (relatively smooth coastline), Most devastating tsunamis, Relatively few islands, A ‘sea’ of monsoons

Southern Ocean

Historical names: Southern Icy Ocean, Antarctic Ocean

Area: ≈ 20,327,000 km² · Volume: ≈ 72,370,000 km³

Max depth: 8,264 m · Avg depth: 3,270 m · Salinity: 34–34.2‰

Temperament / Climate: Marine polar and Antarctic

Distinguishing features: Fully open to the global ocean system, Seasonal fluctuations in sea-ice extent, Located entirely in the Southern Hemisphere, Home to the world’s most powerful ocean current, crossing all meridians, Planet’s primary heat reservoir and carbon sink, Highest concentration of icebergs, Deep continental shelves

Arctic Ocean

Historical names: Frigid Sea, Northern Ocean (Oceanus Septentrionalis), Ocean Sea, Hyperborean Ocean, Frozen Sea (Mare Glaciale), Icy Ocean

Area: ≈ 14,750,000 km² · Volume: ≈ 18,000,000 km³

Max depth: 5,527 m · Avg depth: 1,225 m · Salinity: ~30‰

Temperament / Climate: Subarctic, with Arctic and temperate influences

Distinguishing features: Surrounds the North Pole, Severely restricted water exchange with other oceans, Lowest surface salinity of any ocean, Smallest ocean on Earth, Shallowest ocean on Earth, Vast continental shelves, Extensive floating sea ice

地球上所有大洋

海洋值得人们如同描写人类一样去书写它。欧内斯特・海明威

太平洋

曾用名: 南大洋、东方大洋、大洋

面积: ≈ 178,680,000 平方千米 · 体积: ≈ 710,360,000 立方千米

最大深度: 11,022 米 · 平均深度: 3,976 米 · 盐度: 32–36.5 ‰

地球上面积最大、深度最大的大洋。大量火山岛与珊瑚岛，环太平洋火山地震带，记录到海啸次数最多，地球最深处。

大西洋

面积: ≈ 91,660,000 平方千米 · 体积: ≈ 329,660,000 立方千米

最大深度: 8,742 米 · 平均深度: 3,597 米 · 盐度: 35.3 ‰

面积和深度第二。绵延最长的洋中脊，最高的潮汐波（芬迪湾），大洋中盐度最高，大型上升流。

印度洋

面积: ≈ 76,000,000 平方千米 · 体积: ≈ 280,000,000 立方千米

最大深度: 7,729 米 · 平均深度: 3,700–3,900 米 · 盐度: 30–36 ‰

大部分位于南半球。最长的珊瑚岛链，表层水温高，季风之海，最具破坏性的海啸。

南大洋

面积: ≈ 20,327,000 平方千米 · 体积: ≈ 72,370,000 立方千米

最大深度: 8,264 米 · 平均深度: 3,270 米 · 盐度: 34–34.2 ‰

完全接入全球大洋环流系统。全球最强的洋流，地球主要储热库与碳汇，冰山数量最多。

北冰洋

面积: ≈ 14,750,000 平方千米 · 体积: ≈ 18,000,000 立方千米

最大深度: 5,527 米 · 平均深度: 1,225 米 · 盐度: ~30 ‰

面积最小、平均水深最浅。环绕北极点，表层海水盐度全球最低，大陆架范围广袤，浮冰分布广泛。
