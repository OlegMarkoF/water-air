The Ocean of the Titan Atlas

Leaving behind the perilous yet breathtaking realm of the Indian Ocean, we arrive at our next stop — the waters of the Atlantic. Relatively narrow, this ocean stretches like a winding ribbon from pole to pole, bordering Earth’s largest continents.

Its coastline is deeply indented, and its complex seafloor features relatively few islands, guyots, and coral reefs. Running down the centre of the ocean is the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, a rift valley where magma wells up to form new oceanic crust, triggering frequent earthquakes and volcanic eruptions. Major river systems — including the Amazon, Mississippi, St. Lawrence, Congo, and Danube — discharge roughly 45% of all global river runoff into the Atlantic.

The ocean is renowned for icebergs calving from the Greenland Ice Sheet and for powerful tropical cyclones. Its waters are crisscrossed by hundreds of currents, most notably the Gulf Stream, to which Europe owes its mild climate. Here, the world’s highest tides occur, waves can appear to stand still, and the first scientifically recorded giant solitary waves — known as rogue waves (or freak waves) — were documented. It is also the site of maritime history’s most famous disaster: the sinking of the British ocean liner Titanic.

The Atlantic is the most thoroughly explored and heavily utilized of all oceans, playing a pivotal role in the global economy. Its vast potential is harnessed across shipping, commercial fisheries, mineral extraction, submarine communications, energy, and recreation.

  • Until the 16th century, the word ‘ocean’ was not applied to the Atlantic
  • Christopher Columbus was the first European navigator to venture across the Atlantic Ocean
  • Its deepest point is the Puerto Rico Trench
  • It is home to the Gulf Stream, the warmest major ocean current
  • It contains the Sargasso Sea — the only sea on Earth with no land boundaries
  • The infamous Bermuda Triangle is located within its waters
  • Massive dust storms sweep across the ocean from the Sahara
  • It features the Great Blue Hole, a giant marine karst sinkhole measuring 300 m across and 124 m deep
  • According to legend, the sunken city of Atlantis lies on its seafloor

泰坦阿特拉斯之海（大西洋）

领略过既凶险又风光绮丽的印度洋，我们来到下一站 —— 大西洋海域。这片大洋相对狭窄，如同一条蜿蜒长带纵贯两极，环绕着地球上几大块大陆。

大西洋海岸线十分曲折；海底地形复杂，岛屿、海底平顶山以及珊瑚礁数量相对稀少。大洋中部横亘着大西洋中脊，裂谷带内岩浆喷涌，生成新生地壳，同时伴随地震与火山喷发。亚马逊河、密西西比河、圣劳伦斯河、刚果河、多瑙河等巨型水系汇入大西洋，全球约45%的河流径流量都注入这片大洋。

大西洋因格陵兰冰盖崩解脱落的冰山与热带气旋而闻名。海域内分布着数百条洋流。墨西哥湾暖流在此奔涌，欧洲温和的气候便得益于此；这里拥有世界最高潮差，还会出现 “驻波”；人类首次在此记录到巨型怪浪（疯狗浪）；著名的海难事件 —— 英国跨大西洋邮轮“泰坦尼克号”沉没也发生于此。

大西洋是人类研究程度最高、开发最充分的大洋，在世界经济中占据举足轻重的地位。它的价值体现在航运、渔业、矿产开采、通信系统、能源产业与休闲旅游各个领域。

  • 直到16世纪，“大洋”一词才用于指代大西洋
  • 哥伦布是首位敢于横渡大西洋的航海家
  • 最深处——波多黎各海沟
  • 最温暖的洋流——墨西哥湾流
  • 没有海岸的海——马尾藻海
  • 百慕大三角坐落于此
  • 沙尘暴在此肆虐
  • 大蓝洞——喀斯特落水洞，直径300米，深度124米
  • 传说水下城市亚特兰蒂斯沉睡在大西洋海底
