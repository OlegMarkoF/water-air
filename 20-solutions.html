What May I Hope For? — Solutions

Why has humanity travelled such a long and arduous path to understand the ocean and the atmosphere? To protect ourselves and build a safer, more liveable world. Every scientific inquiry aims to improve human well-being, and the ultimate goal of our expedition across the planet’s oceans is to answer one of life’s most profound questions: “What may I hope for?”

Today, our ocean and atmosphere face severe threats — from municipal and industrial waste, rising temperatures, and sea-level rise to oil slicks, greenhouse gases, species extinction, and the decline of coral reefs. Yet these challenges have tangible solutions. Innovators are developing biodegradable materials and autonomous waste-collecting robots; marine scientists are cultivating resilient coral species and transplanting them onto degraded reefs; and marine protected areas are helping entire ecosystems recover. Industrial facilities are adopting advanced filtration systems and carbon capture technologies, such as hybrid power plants. Proven strategies also play a vital role — including urban greening, which absorbs carbon dioxide and mitigates urban heat, alongside wind and hydropower technologies capable of powering entire cities. In Denmark, for instance, wind turbines generate nearly half of the nation’s domestic electricity. Equally essential are international conventions and environmental laws that regulate human impact through strict standards for emissions, wastewater treatment, and habitat conservation.

Above all, we must remember that even small actions add up to meaningful change.

Only by combining science, technology, policy and personal responsibility can we effectively safeguard our planet.

  • Wind power, sailing propulsion

  • Hydrogen fuel, atmospheric electricity

我可以怀抱何种希望？——解决路径

人类耗费漫长艰难的历程去认知海洋与大气，意义何在？为了保障人类自身安全，让世界变得更加宜居。一切研究都旨在改善人类生存条件。我们这场星球海洋考察，最终要回答每个人生命中最重要的问题之一：我可以抱有何种希望？

海洋与大气正面临严峻威胁：生活与工业废弃物、气温与海平面上升、油膜污染、温室气体、物种消亡、珊瑚礁退化。但这些难题拥有现实可行的解决方案：科研人员研发可生物降解材料、垃圾回收机器人；培育抗逆珊瑚品种，移植到受损珊瑚礁；自然保护区助力完整生态系统修复。企业安装过滤装置、二氧化碳捕集技术，例如混合动力电站。一些经过实践检验的方法同样意义重大：城市绿化，吸收二氧化碳、降低环境温度；风能、水能技术可以为整座城市供电 —— 丹麦风力发电几乎占到本国发电量的一半。各类国际公约与法律法规同样发挥重要作用，规范人类对环境的影响：排放、净化、保护相关标准等等。

重要的是要记住：即使是微小的行动也会汇聚产生宏大效果。
唯有科学、技术、法规制度与个人责任相结合，才是守护地球的有效道路。
