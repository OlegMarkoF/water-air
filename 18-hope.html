What May I Hope For?

It is not nature that needs our protection; rather, it is we who need nature’s stewardship: clean air to breathe, crystal-clear water to drink, and all of nature simply to live. Nikolai Reimers

Ocean Crises Are Human Crises

On our planet, all living and non-living elements interact with one another. Every action we take triggers a response in the natural world. We must consider how to adapt to the consequences of natural processes while finding ways to reduce our anthropogenic impact on the environment.

Industrial development is driven by the massive consumption of natural resources and the generation of vast amounts of waste, leading to severe pollution of both the ocean and the atmosphere. These two Earth spheres are in constant interaction, meaning shifts in one inevitably alter the other — making their pollution deeply interconnected.

Our atmosphere is suffocating from rising greenhouse gas concentrations, while the ‘grasshopper effect’ — also known as global distillation — carries pollutants released in temperate and tropical regions all the way to the poles. We are already feeling the effects of shifting ozone holes, persistent smog, acid rain, and rising levels of light, thermal, and even electromagnetic pollution.

The scarcity of clean, potable freshwater is one of the most pressing global challenges of our time. Water is the foundational resource sustaining life on Earth, yet its uneven distribution across continents, declining quality, and salinization have repeatedly triggered humanitarian crises and social upheaval. Furthermore, how much do we truly understand about noise pollution, thermal discharge, and our carbon footprint?

Water and air quality = quality of life

Harmonious interaction with the oceans is the only sustainable path forward for humanity.

海洋问题即人类的问题

在我们的星球上，一切生命与非生命事物都会在一定程度上相互影响。人类尤其如此，人类的任何行为都会引发环境的反馈。我们应当思考，如何接纳自然进程带来的后果，以及应当做些什么，来降低人为活动给环境带来的负荷。

人类文明的工业化发展伴随着自然资源的巨大消耗，同时产生大量废弃物，造成海洋与大气污染。地球的这两大圈层持续相互作用，其中一方指标发生变化，就会引发另一方随之改变，因此二者的污染具备交叉影响的特点。

大气这一“空气海洋”因温室气体含量升高而不堪重负。“蚱蜢效应”，也叫全球蒸馏效应，会把中纬度、热带地区排放的污染物输送至极地并造成危害。人类正饱受臭氧空洞范围与位置变化、光化学烟雾蔓延、酸雨、光污染、热污染乃至电磁污染加剧带来的困扰。

洁净的饮用淡水短缺，是当代全球性难题之一。水是维系地球生命最重要的资源，但各大洲水资源分布不均、水质恶化、水体盐化，已多次引发危机与社会灾难。而对于噪声污染、热排放以及碳足迹，我们又了解多少？

水与空气的质量 = 生活的质量
