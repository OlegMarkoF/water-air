The Cloud Atlas

A cloud is not a thing, but a process. Heinrich Dove

From the deck of a ship, clouds offer an extraordinary view. At times they drift slowly like delicate feathers; at others, they hang overhead as heavy, leaden storm clouds. The formation of each individual cloud is shaped by numerous factors, every one of which leaves its own distinct imprint on the cloud’s appearance. Cloud formations and the precipitation they release rank among the most important atmospheric phenomena influencing our daily lives. The International Cloud Atlas recognizes ten principal cloud genera, classifying them by their shape, size, structure, colour, transparency, and the type and intensity of precipitation they produce.

What can clouds tell us? Will today bring clear, fair weather — or should you grab an umbrella before heading out for a walk because a downpour is on the way?

云图图集

云，不是物体，而是一种过程。 海因里希・多夫

站在轮船甲板上看云，会看到别样景致。云朵时而如同轻柔羽毛缓缓飘荡，时而化作沉重灰暗的乌云沉沉压下。一朵云的形成受诸多因素共同作用，每一种因素都会在云的外观上留下印记。云系以及伴随产生的降水，是影响我们日常生活最重要的自然现象之一。国际云分类体系划分出十种主要云型，分类依据包含云的轮廓、尺寸、结构、颜色、透明度，还有降水的类型与特征。

云能够向我们传递哪些信息？今天会是晴空万里，还是出门需要带上雨伞，因为大雨转瞬即至？
