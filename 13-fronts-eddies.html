Ocean Fronts: Where the Ocean Changes Its “Character”

Across the ocean there are regions where distinct water masses collide or transition into one another. The boundaries between them are called ocean fronts — acting like invisible “walls” or transition zones. A front is a sloping boundary surface across which temperature, salinity, density and other properties change abruptly. These regions foster unique marine environments and are extraordinarily rich in marine life. The strongest fronts typically stretch along the edges of major ocean currents. Dynamic fronts also form where surface currents converge or diverge.

Moving in Circles: Ocean Eddies

During the 1970 Soviet Polygon-70 expedition in the tropical North Atlantic, researchers observed rotating water structures measuring 100–150 km across. This marked the discovery of mesoscale ocean eddies. Ranging from tens to hundreds of kilometres in diameter and extending hundreds or even thousands of metres deep, these eddies influence upwelling, heat and moisture exchange with the atmosphere, and weather patterns.

Frontal eddies (ocean rings) form when meanders pinch off from major currents. The most famous are Gulf Stream rings — massive eddies 100–300 km across that can persist for several years before remerging with the current that spawned them.

海洋锋面：海洋“特性”发生转变的地带

海洋中存在水团相遇或相互过渡的地方。它们的边界称为锋面——它们类似于看不见的“墙壁”或过渡带。锋面是一个倾斜的表面，温度、盐度、密度和其他水体特征在此急剧变化。锋面对科研人员极具研究价值，这里会形成独特海洋环境。渔民也不会忽视它们，因为这些区域生命极其丰富。最强的锋面通常沿着主要洋流的边界延伸。

环形运动：海洋涡旋

在北大西洋热带区域，苏联“多边形-70”实验（1970年）期间，专家们发现了大小100–150公里的水体结构。就这样，人们发现了海洋中的天气涡旋。涡旋直径数十至数百千米，垂直延伸数百至上千米。涡旋会带动深层水上涌，改变海气之间热量、水汽交换，影响区域天气。

最知名的就是墨西哥湾暖流环涡。洋流流过哈特拉斯角之后发生剧烈蛇曲，会不时分裂出巨型海洋环涡，直径100‑300千米；有的可以独立存在数年，之后重新汇入原洋流。
