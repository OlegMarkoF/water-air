Remarkable Anomalies

Water is H₂O, hydrogen two parts, oxygen one, but there is also a third thing, that makes it water and nobody knows what it is. D. H. Lawrence

Water is one of the most extraordinary substances known to humanity. Over 40 unusual properties set it apart from most other liquids. We have grown so accustomed to these traits that we scarcely notice them, yet they drive some of the most vital processes on Earth: they transport nutrients to the tops of the tallest trees, protect aquatic life by preventing lakes and rivers from freezing solid to the bottom, and enable ocean currents to warm northern regions by carrying heat from tropical latitudes.

Water is the only naturally occurring substance found in abundance in all three states of matter — solid, liquid, and gas. Among liquids, it possesses the highest surface tension, dielectric constant, and heat of vapourization, alongside an exceptionally high heat of fusion. Because water is a universal solvent, the World Ocean contains nearly every element on the periodic table.

Every scientific hypothesis, conclusion, or claim must be backed not merely by words, but by calculations and experiments — wouldn’t you agree?

Key questions explored in the laboratory

Water: A Giant Polymer

Water is far more than a simple collection of molecules; it is a complex, continuously shifting system. Its exact structure remains an active frontier of research. Researchers agree that the foundation is an extensive network of hydrogen bonds. One of the most compelling hypotheses is the cluster model proposed by George Némethy and Harold A. Scheraga in 1962.

Should Ice Float in Water?

While virtually all substances sink in their own liquid form when solid, ice floats because it is less dense. As water cools to 3.98 °C it becomes denser; further cooling causes it to expand.

What Is the Triple Point of Water?

The specific conditions under which water can coexist simultaneously in all three phases — vapour, liquid, and solid. At 611.73 Pa the boiling and melting points converge at exactly 0.01 °C.

Why Are the Oceans Heat Regulators?

Water’s anomalously high heat capacity turns the seas and oceans into gigantic planetary thermostats: they absorb heat during the day and release that stored warmth at night, preventing extreme temperature swings.

奇妙的反常特性

水的分子式是H₂O，即两个氢原子，一个氧原子。但还有第三种东西，将这些原子凝聚成为水，而没人知道那究竟是什么。 戴维・赫伯特・劳伦斯

水是人类已知最神奇的物质之一。它拥有四十余种非同寻常的特性，区别于绝大多数液体。我们对此早已习以为常，甚至熟视无睹，但正是这些特性构成地球上诸多关键过程的基础：把营养物质输送至树木顶端；让水生生物免于死亡，水体不会彻底封冻至湖底；大洋洋流将南方的热量输送过来，温暖地球诸多北方区域。

水是唯一一种能够大量以固态、液态、气态三种物态同时存在于自然界的物质。它拥有极高的表面张力、介电常数、汽化热，熔化热数值也很高。水是万能溶剂，因此门捷列夫元素周期表中绝大多数元素都能在世界大洋中找到。

实验室中探讨的关键问题

水——巨型聚合物

水不只是无数分子的简单集合，而是一套复杂、持续变化的系统。水的结构至今仍是科研热点。整套结构的基础是贯穿全部水分子的氢键网络。颇具影响力的假说是乔治・内梅蒂与哈罗德・谢拉吉于1962年提出的液态水团簇结构模型。

冰应该浮在水上吗？

虽然几乎所有物质在固态下都会沉入其液体中，但冰因密度较小而浮在水上。水在冷却至+3.98°C时逐渐变密、体积减小，但进一步降温则导致体积增大。

水的三相点是什么？

这是水可以同时以三种相态——蒸汽、液体和固体——存在的条件。当压强为611.73帕，水的沸点与熔点重合，温度等于0.01 ℃。

为什么海洋是热量调节器？

水拥有异常高的比热容，使得海洋成为地球巨型温控调节器：白天海水吸热，夜晚释放储存的热量，以此避免气温出现剧烈起伏。
