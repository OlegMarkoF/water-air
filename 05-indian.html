The Ocean of Warm Waters

To the west of the Pacific lies the Indian Ocean. Most of it spans tropical and subtropical climate zones, with only its southern reaches experiencing the cooling influence of Antarctica. Its seafloor is marked by numerous shallow submarine plateaus and ridges, yet the ocean contains relatively few islands. Several of the world’s largest rivers — the Indus, Ganges, and Brahmaputra — empty into the Indian Ocean basin, discharging roughly 1.3 billion metric tonnes of sediment each year. Its northeastern waters lie within seismically active zones that have witnessed some of the most devastating tsunamis in history.

The waters of the Indian Ocean are renowned for their clarity and deep azure-blue hue. They are also slightly saltier than those of most other oceans — a trait especially pronounced in the northwest, where the hot breath of the Sahara drives intense evaporation. Because of its high sea surface temperatures (averaging 17–20 °C), the Indian Ocean is known as the Ocean of Warm Waters. Another fitting moniker is the Sea of Monsoons, as seasonal monsoon winds govern the currents across its northern basin.

Overall, the Indian Ocean has relatively low biological productivity, accounting for only 5–7% of the global marine fisheries catch. While many of its abundant natural resources remain underutilized, its offshore basins rank first in the world for oil and gas reserves and production. Its coastal zone is exceptionally densely populated and is recognized as a cradle of ancient civilizations. Today, more than 35 nations border the Indian Ocean.

  • Considered the youngest and warmest of Earth’s oceans
  • Home to the Red Sea, the second-saltiest sea in the world
  • Indonesia, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Réunion, India, and the Maldives — surfer’s paradise
  • Red Sea and Persian Gulf — historic centres for pearls and mother-of-pearl
  • In 1938, the coelacanth (Latimeria) was discovered here
  • Over 30% of the world’s population lives along its coasts and islands

暖水大洋

在世界第一大洋（太平洋）的西侧，便是印度洋。印度洋主体地处温暖气候带，仅南部海域受南极影响。大洋海底分布众多小型海底高原与海岭，但岛屿数量稀少。世界几条大河 —— 印度河、恒河、布拉马普特拉河均汇入印度洋流域，每年带入约13亿吨沉积物。印度洋东北部地处地震活跃带，历史上曾发生破坏力极强的海啸。

印度洋海水以高透明度、深蓝‑蔚蓝色著称，海水盐度略高于其他大洋。西北部受撒哈拉沙漠干热气流影响，盐度尤为突出。由于表层水温高（+17…20°C），印度洋被称为“暖水大洋”。它还有一个别称 —— 季风之海。北部洋流受季风支配。春秋两季北部会生成台风，给孟加拉湾、阿拉伯海沿岸带来巨大灾害。

印度洋生物生产力不高，鱼类和海产品捕捞量仅占全球捕捞量的5–7%。丰富的自然资源利用不足，但该水域的石油和天然气储量及开采量居世界第一。印度洋沿岸地区人口密度极高，被认为是古代文明发源地之一。如今沿岸分布35个以上国家。

  • 被公认为最年轻、水温最高的大洋
  • 红海盐度位居世界第二
  • 印度尼西亚、斯里兰卡、毛里求斯、留尼汪、印度、马尔代夫 —— 冲浪爱好者的天堂
  • 珍珠与珍珠母贝的主要产地：红海、波斯湾
  • 1938年发现古老鱼类（腔棘鱼目）代表物种 —— 矛尾鱼
  • 沿岸及岛屿居住着全球30%以上人口
